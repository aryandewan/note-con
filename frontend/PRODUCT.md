# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Multiplayer gamers across the spectrum — ranked grinders who need reliable,
skill-matched teammates *now*, and casual co-op players who just want good
company for the evening. Context: at home, often in the moments before a play
session, deciding whether tonight actually happens. The job to be done: turn
"I want to play with people" into a full, ready squad with the least friction —
either by jumping into something live or by scheduling a session others can
join until the slots fill.

## Product Purpose

Rally is where you find your squad. Hosts open a session (game, vibe, size,
time); players browse open squads and claim open slots until the squad is full
and the game kicks off. Success = a player goes from landing on the page to a
full squad fast, and a host's open slots reliably fill without no-shows. The
landing page's job is to make that feel immediate and alive — the proof is open
squads filling up in real time, not a feature list.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, social, and welcoming — a bright arena, not a dark esports bunker.
Three words: **fast, friendly, alive.** It speaks to a broad crowd, so tone is
flexible: confident without being try-hard, warm without being soft. Lets each
squad set its own intensity (ranked-serious or just-vibing) rather than
prescribing one. Emotional goal: the buzz of a lobby filling up — anticipation,
belonging, "let's go."

## Anti-references

- The default dark-neon-esports look: black backgrounds, violet/cyan glow,
  angular "gamer" chrome, RGB everything. Overdone and excludes the casual half
  of the audience.
- Generic AI-purple-on-white SaaS landing pages.
- The hero-metric template (three big gradient numbers) and identical
  icon+heading+text card grids.
- Discord/forum density — walls of text, no sense of motion or life.
- Faking copyrighted game box art as hero imagery.

## Design Principles

- **Show the lobby filling, don't describe it.** The hero is live, real-feeling
  open squads with slots that fill — the product's value visible at a glance.
- **Light and loud.** Brightness and bold color carry the energy; the surface
  stays crisp so squad data is always legible.
- **Everyone's invited.** Copy and visuals never assume hardcore-only; the
  squad, not the brand, sets the intensity.
- **Motion is the message.** Filling meters, a live activity ticker, ticking
  counts — movement communicates "alive right now," so it's purposeful, never
  decorative.
- **Legible under color.** Bold fields are fine; text and the all-important
  slot counts must always clear contrast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large/bold ≥3:1, slot counts and status
never conveyed by color alone (paired with labels, fractions, icons). Full
keyboard paths and visible focus. Every animation (filling meters, ticker,
count-ups, reveals) has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative that shows final
state instantly. Light-only theme, chosen deliberately for the bright-arena
mood.
